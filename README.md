## 📚 What it does

| Feature | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| 📄 **Upload notes** | PDF, Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx) or a photo of your notes |
| 🧠 **AI summary** | A short, clear summary plus the key concepts and definitions |
| 📝 **Practice quizzes** | Multiple-choice questions with explanations for every answer |
| 📅 **Study calendar** | A day-by-day study plan built around your exam date |
| 💬 **Ask AI** | Ask questions about your notes and get answers based only on them |
| 🎯 **Exam focus** | The topics most likely to matter, plus a memory trick for the hardest one |

## 🚀 How to use it

1. Go to **[studystudentai.github.io/studentstudyai-web](https://studystudentai.github.io/studentstudyai-web/)**
2. Tap **Sign up** and make a free account
3. Upload your notes from the **Documents** page
4. Read your summary, take the quiz and follow your study calendar

**On Android:** download the APK from **[Releases](https://github.com/StudyStudentAI/studentstudyai-web/releases/latest)**, open it on your phone to install, and log in with the same account. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser. The app updates itself automatically.

**On iPhone:** open the website in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
